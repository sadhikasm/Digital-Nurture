The City Council has announced several exciting community events this month. Residents are encouraged to participate in the annual Food Festival, Sports Day, and Tree Plantation Drive. These events aim to bring people together and promote a healthy and active lifestyle. The Food Festival will feature local restaurants and home chefs showcasing a variety of dishes. Sports Day includes games and competitions for all age groups. The Tree Plantation Drive focuses on environmental awareness and creating greener public spaces. Registration for all events is open through the Community Event Portal. Participants can check event schedules, locations, and updates online. Community members are invited to volunteer and contribute to making these events successful and enjoyable for everyone.
